## 4. Findings

### 4.1 — Earnings calendar unavailable for 4 consecutive sessions → **#507**

`giorno_di_earnings` is `None` (UNKNOWN, not `False`) on **100% of S4 intents** on 08-31, 09-01,
09-02 and 09-03 — 5.458 intents. It worked and discriminated until 08-27 (flagged `True` on 24
NVDA intents on 08-26). Worst case 09-02, where the three largest movers of the universe were
all earnings reactions and the #335 earnings qualifier was blind on every intent.

**Root cause pinned during this review.** `scripts/daily_alpha_miss_analysis.sh` sources `.env`
selectively (lines 42 and 72) and whitelists only `ALPACA_*` and `TELEGRAM_*`. `FMP_API_KEY` is
in `.env` but never exported, so `_corporate_calendar()` takes its no-key branch every run.
`git log -S FMP_API_KEY -- scripts/daily_alpha_miss_analysis.sh` is empty: it was never wired.
The earlier working dossiers were generated inside agent sessions that had sourced the full file.
Secondary: the no-key and HTTP-failure branches emit the identical `earnings_calendar_unavailable`
marker, so a config bug is indistinguishable from an outage. Tertiary, verified live with the
`.env` key (HTTP 200): FMP `stable/earnings-calendar` returns `['NIO']` for 09-01, `[]` for 09-02
and `['DOCU']` for 09-03 — the source is thin for this watchlist even when reachable.

### 4.2 — Content-mill articles pass `ISSUER_SPECIFIC` with zero catalyst → **#508**

GS closed +3,34% on 09-03 with two articles, both empty: «If You Invested $100 In Goldman Sachs
Group Stock 15 Years Ago…» (`ISSUER_SPECIFIC`, score **0,002**) and a whale-activity listicle
(0,000). The entity tag is *correct* — that is what distinguishes this from #405. Same template
on ORCL 09-01 («$100 Invested In Oracle 20 Years Ago…», score 0,000, `max_score_own = 0,000` on a
−5,23% day) and BABA 08-31. `_classify_relevance` returns `ISSUER_SPECIFIC` on mere entity
presence in the text, with no content test, and `effective_timely` counts the result as coverage —
so every published coverage figure is inflated by the filler share.

### 4.3 — `NON_CLASSIFICATO` swallows above-gate rejects and drops them from the cost ledger → **#509**

PLTR 09-02 (score −0,420, correct sign, above gate, killed by `SKIP_FALLBACK`, 15 rows) and
TSLA 09-03 (score 0,468, above gate, no decision row at all) both land in `NON_CLASSIFICATO`.
`funnel_v2` in the *same dossier* resolves both correctly as `FALLBACK_REJECT`. The legacy field
is the one that feeds `aggregati.cause_del_giorno` → `market_daily.jsonl` → `_miss_cumulati()` →
the charter's exit-question-1 falsification criterion, 24 days into a 40-day window. Worse,
`panels.py:48` lists `NON_CLASSIFICATO` in `NON_OCCORRENZA`, so these candidates generate **no
cost occurrence at all**.

Note for the record: `ALPHA_MISS_REPORT_2026-09-01.md` declared this defect apparently fixed
because the category was absent that day. It reappeared the next session. It was never fixed —
it was **without cases**.

### 4.4 — Evidence pipeline commits unvalidated JSON and continues on a broken ledger → **#510**

On 09-02 the cron opened on a `findings.json` containing unresolved `git stash pop` conflict
markers, logged `RIFIUTO` + `ATTENZIONE`, and **ran to completion and pushed** (`GIT_STATUS=pushed`,
exit 0). Recovery revealed 12 occurrences from 2026-08-13 that had never been committed anywhere in
repo history, and a bifurcated id (`F-039` meaning two different findings), renumbered to `F-060`.

Two mechanisms remain: `refresh_evidence_ledger.sh` warns and exits 0 on an unmergeable ledger;
and `commit_evidence_ledger.sh::stage_paths()` validates `findings.json` and `*.jsonl` via the merge
scripts but falls through to a bare `cp` for everything else — which is exactly the path taken by
`docs/evidence/dossier/*.json`. A dossier with conflict markers would have been committed verbatim.

**Current state verified 2026-09-05: both files parse, `prossimo_id: 67` / 66 findings, and
`git stash list` is empty.** The incident is closed; the mechanisms are not.

### 4.5 — The news blind spot is per-ticker and permanent, not a rotating daily gap → **#511**

Measured across all 8 committed dossiers 2026-08-24 → 2026-09-03 (08-28 absent, F-044/#396):

- **16 of 96 symbols (16,7%) had zero articles on _every_ session**: ASML, AZN, BP, ERIC, GE, IBM,
  JD, MMM, PBR, RDDT, RIO, SAP, SBUX, SONY, T, VALE.
- **40 of 96 (41,7%) had zero `effective_timely` issuer-specific articles across all 8** —
  including IBM, SAP, NFLX, JNJ, V, COST, CVX.

Every Week 36 NO_NEWS miss comes from that 16-name set, including the week's two largest accessible
opportunities (RDDT 60,19 $, ARM 132,54 $). The set skews heavily to non-US issuers and ADRs,
consistent with a coverage hole in the two sources that supply everything (`alpaca_benzinga` 69%,
`gdelt_gkg`; source HHI 0,504). Exit-side face: ASML at **10+ consecutive sessions** with
`fonti_osservate_finestra` empty while −6,93% from entry.

The most uncomfortable single fact of the week sits here: on 09-01 **both upside movers were
captured and neither capture came from the sentiment pipeline** — PBR and BP had zero news rows and
were held/entered by S1 momentum, which does not read news. Had they depended on S4 they would have
been two NO_NEWS misses.

### 4.6 — S4 buys after the session move is more than 100% complete → **#512**

NVDA 09-02 `quota_movimento_precedente_al_segnale` **1,0676** (`denominatore_degenere: false`),

Mirror image on the miss side, confirming the metric measures something real: 6 of the 8 misses on
09-03 had **negative** `net_opportunity_usd` — even with every gate disarmed, by the first eligible
cycle the move was gone.

## 8. Reading of the week

Three claims the evidence supports, and one it does not.

**The pipeline's binding constraint this week was input supply, not scoring.** 16 symbols are
permanently invisible; 40 have never produced a timely issuer-specific article; the two best
captures of 09-01 came from a momentum sleeve that does not read news at all. Gate tuning cannot
reach any of that.

**Where scoring did engage, lateness usually destroyed the opportunity before any gate rejected it.**
Six of eight misses on 09-03 had *negative* accessible opportunity. Two entries were placed after
the session move was more than 100% complete. The near-gate misses that look most damning (0,019,
0,023) mostly had no money behind them.

**The measurement instruments failed silently three separate ways this week** — earnings calendar
blind for four sessions, a cron pushing on a corrupt ledger, above-gate rejects excluded from cost
accounting — during the exact window whose only product is measurement. Four of the six issues
filed are instrument repairs, and that ratio is the finding.

**What the evidence does not support:** that S4's ≈−400 $ economic result is a verdict on the
signal. It is a verdict on a pipeline that could not see a sixth of its universe, bought late, and
exited on confidence it would refuse for an entry. The n=73 kill criterion arrives in about three
sessions (#179) and should be pre-registered with that stated, not after the number is visible.
