## Result

`ACA006`, `ACA009`, `MET003`, `MET005`, and `IND001`. No sufficiently reliable
and currently retrievable full-text alternative was verified for `ACA005`,
`MET006`, or `NEW001`; those entries are deliberately marked `not found`
rather than being replaced by merely related work.

`verified` means that the alternative returned HTTP 200 on 2026-09-01 and its
title/authors plus DOI, publication details, or EDGAR accession matched the
manifested document. `not found` means that identity metadata was verified but
no full text suitable for automated ingestion was verified as retrievable. It
does not assert that no copy exists anywhere.
